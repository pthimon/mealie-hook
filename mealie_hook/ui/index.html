      Export

      One line per item, for pasting into the supermarket's list search. Crossed-out Home Assistant items are already covered by a Mealie item.

      Recipes flagged for review

      Written as far as was safe; each reason says what to check. "Mark reviewed" removes the tag and the note, nothing else.

      Prompts

      The instructions sent to Qwen. Placeholders like {{tags}} are filled from the vocabulary above; "What Qwen sees" shows the result.

      Change the rules

      Describe a change in plain words, e.g. "add Duck as a protein tag", "stop tagging traybakes Summer", "tofu goes in Chilled & Deli". Qwen proposes an edit; nothing changes until you press Apply, and every change can be reverted from History.
